Conventions, themes and program chairs

Every CCCC convention from 1950 on, with its dates, site, theme and program chair as NCTE lists them. For the years in the corpus (1998 on), the last column asks a simple question: how often do the words of the theme turn up in that year's session and paper titles, compared with the other years?

Dates, sites, themes and chairs come from NCTE's page on past conventions ({{ n_theme }} themes are listed; the first appears in {{ first_theme }}, and the page has none for 2026 or 2027 yet). The echo is a rough measure. It counts titles that use any distinctive word from the theme (words in more than 3% of all titles, like “writing,” are left out), a word matches from its start (“gateway” finds “gateways”), and a theme's words may be ordinary in the field. Read it as a hint, then open the titles.
