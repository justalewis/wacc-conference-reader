Who convenes the field?

Each year's convention program lists the people CCCC put on its stages: who chaired, who spoke, who responded, and from which institutions. This page counts them, year by year, from 1998 to 2027.

These are people listed in the program, not registrants or attendees. 2000–2003 is missing, and the source changes from PDF to eShow in 2024. Shaded bands on the chart mark those breaks. How to read these numbers.
