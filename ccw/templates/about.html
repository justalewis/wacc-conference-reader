About

This reader covers the printed programs of the Conference on Community Writing from 2015 to 2025. The sources are the program PDFs for 2015 and 2017, the schedule page on the Coalition for Community Writing's site for 2019, the public program documents for 2021 and 2023, and the public DeepThink Tank listing for 2025. It follows the design of the CCCC Program Reader and shares its tools where the data allows.

The programs belong to the Coalition for Community Writing, and the reader is built with its permission. Abstracts and session descriptions are not shown or exported. It is a draft: several years need review before the figures can be cited, and 2025 is only partly public. The archive selector links to the other readers as well.
