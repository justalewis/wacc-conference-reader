Panel network and teams

Who shares sessions with whom. The network joins people who were listed in the same session, in two windows (2015 to 2019 and 2021 to 2025) and across all six editions.

Thresholds. Only people listed at two or more conferences appear, and a line needs two shared sessions. Sessions with more than twelve of these people are left out. The graph keeps the 220 best-connected people in each window and is laid out by software, so a person's position says little. The heavily OCR'd programs (1985, 1997, 2007, 2008 and 2012) are not used for person pages, and a person appears only under an exact printed name, so two spellings split into two nodes. No one is ranked or labeled. See ethics.
