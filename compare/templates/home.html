Synkrisis

Synkrisis is the ancient rhetorical exercise of setting two subjects side by side. Here it sets the conference archives side by side: the same measures for every series, on the same years, with each series' gaps left visible. Pick a comparison from the sidebar.

These are people listed in a program, not attendees. The series differ in size by a factor of thirty, so every page leads with shares and rates, and counts are given beside them. No page names a person.

The comparisons

ChronikonAny measure from the yearly census, every series on one set of years: size, newcomers, institutions printed, concentration.
OikoiWhich schools appear in which archives, how much each archive leans on its top schools, and how alike the lists are.
TopoiOne word or phrase across every archive's titles, the year it takes off in each, and the words that set an archive apart.
ProsopographiaHow many people are listed in more than one archive, which archive they appear in first, as counts only.
