About

This reader covers the annual proceedings of the Council for Programs in Technical and Scientific Communication (CPTSC) from 1974 to 2025, 50 volumes with no 1976 and no 2020, taken from the PDFs on cptsc.org. Some volumes are proceedings with full papers, some are a schedule or an abstract book, and the 1989 volume is an agenda. It follows the design of the CCCC Program Reader and shares its tools where the data allows.

It is a draft. The cptsc.org site states no license, and permission for reuse is being sought from CPTSC. Several years need review before the figures can be cited. Institutions were matched against the CCCC, IWAC and Computers and Writing readers by identical printed form, not through ROR, and the papers' texts and abstracts are not included. The archive selector links to the other readers as well.
