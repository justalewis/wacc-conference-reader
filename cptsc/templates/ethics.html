Ethics

The proceedings are public documents on cptsc.org, and the reader shows only what they print. It still sets limits on how names are used.

  • Person pages exist only for people listed at two or more meetings, not counting 1999. A name that appears once is on its session page and nowhere else.
  • Rights. The cptsc.org site states no license for the volumes. This is a draft, and permission for reuse is being sought from CPTSC.
  • No ranking. The reader never scores or ranks a person, and it shows no attendance.
  • No search indexing. Every page asks search engines not to index it.
  • Papers and abstracts are in the source volumes but are not shown, republished or exported. The reader holds titles, names and institutions only.
  • Errors are likely, especially in the scanned volumes read by OCR (1974 to 2004), above all 1999 (see how to read). A name can be wrong or split in two.
