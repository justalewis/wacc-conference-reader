Who convenes the field?

The Council for Programs in Technical and Scientific Communication (CPTSC) publishes proceedings from its annual meeting. Each volume lists the people the meeting put on its stages: who presented, who moderated or responded, and from which institutions. This page counts them, meeting by meeting, for the 50 volumes in the corpus, 1974 to 2025.

These are people listed in the proceedings, not registrants or attendees. There is no volume for 1976 or 2020. The volumes through 2004 are scans read by OCR and checked by hand against their contents, and 1997 to 2001 print names without institutions in the contents, so institutions come from each paper's byline. In 2009 a third of the listings have no institution, and the 2016 meeting was cancelled for Hurricane Matthew, though its proceedings were published. Shaded bands on the chart mark these breaks.
