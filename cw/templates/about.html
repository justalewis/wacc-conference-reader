About

This reader covers the printed programs of the Computers and Writing Conference from 1984 to 2024. Programs through 2019 come from the PDFs in Are We There Yet? (Marlow and Purdy), published by the Computers and Composition Digital Press; the 2022, 2023 and 2024 programs were supplied by the conference organizers. It follows the design of the CCCC Program Reader and shares its tools where the data allows.

It is a draft. Reuse terms for the data are pending with the Press, whose book carries a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 notice, and with the organizers, who supplied the 2022 to 2024 programs without stating a license, and several years need review before the figures can be cited. The archive selector links to the other readers as well.
