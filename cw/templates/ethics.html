Ethics

The programs are public documents, and the reader shows only what they print. It still sets limits on how names are used.

  • Person pages exist only for people listed at two or more conferences. A name that appears once is on its session page and nowhere else.
  • No ranking. The reader never scores or ranks a person, and it shows no attendance.
  • No search indexing. Every page asks search engines not to index it.
  • Abstracts are in the source programs but are not shown or exported.
  • Errors are likely, especially in the OCR'd programs of 1985, 1997, 2007, 2008 and 2012 (see how to read). A name can be wrong or split in two.
