About

This reader covers the programs of the International Writing Across the Curriculum Conference from 2002 to 2025, as hosted by the WAC Clearinghouse. It follows the design of the CCCC Program Reader and shares its tools where the data allows.

It is a draft. Reuse terms for the data are pending, and several years need review before the figures can be cited. The archive selector links to the CCCC reader as well.
