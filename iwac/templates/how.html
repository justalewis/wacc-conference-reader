How to read these numbers

The reader counts what the printed programs list. It does not count who attended, and it cannot see changes made after the program was printed.

What is counted

  • A listing is one person in one role in one session.
  • People are names joined on an exact match. IWAC has had no reviewed merges, so one person printed with two spellings counts twice.
  • An institution is matched when the printed affiliation maps to a ROR record. The share outside the US uses only matched institutions.
  • A person page exists only for people listed at two or more conferences. The 2002 program does not count toward that, because it is an OCR'd scan.
