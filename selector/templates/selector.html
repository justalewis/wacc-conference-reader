Synkrisis: compare the archives

The same measures for every archive on one set of years: size and newcomers by year, which schools appear where, one word across every archive's titles, and how many people are listed in more than one archive (as counts only, never names).

These are people listed in a program, not attendees. A program is a plan, and every figure is a count of listings. Each reader says where its years are weak.

Tools the readers share

Census by year, institutions, atlas, panel network, search, terms over time, conferences, formats, and a corpus builder. The CCCC reader adds groups, calls and area by institution type, and the IWAC reader adds areas for 2020 and 2025; the Computers and Writing, CPTSC and Community Writing readers have neither.

Nothing here is indexed by search engines. To correct a listing or ask that a page be removed, write to jlewis2@olympic.edu.
