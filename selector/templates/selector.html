Tools the readers share

Census by year, institutions, atlas, panel network, search, terms over time, conferences, formats, and a corpus builder. The CCCC reader adds groups, calls and area by institution type, and the IWAC reader adds areas for 2020 and 2025; the Computers and Writing, CPTSC and Community Writing readers have neither.
